1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how Reverse Image Search (the “App”) handles information when you use our Android application (and iOS, if offered). For privacy questions, contact hello@appomatrix.com.

2. Scope

This policy covers:

  • Using the App to search with camera photos, gallery images, or image URLs
  • Optional AI insights shown in the App
  • Free usage limits, Pro subscriptions, and ads
  • Analytics and push notifications (where enabled)

It does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites that appear in search results. Those sites have their own terms and policies.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

  • Images you select or capture for reverse search or AI insights
  • Image URLs you paste for search
  • In-app actions such as saving favorites or clearing history (stored locally)

3.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device & app diagnostics (for example app version, OS, crash/performance signals via analytics SDKs)
  • Usage events such as app open, search started/completed, subscription screen viewed (Firebase Analytics, when configured)
  • Advertising identifiers used by Google Mobile Ads / AdMob on free tiers (subject to platform limits and your OS settings)
  • Push notification tokens if you allow notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

3.3 Information we do not collect on our own servers

We do not operate a custom user-account database for this App. Search history and favorites are saved on your device. We do not store uploaded images on an App-owned permanent server.

4. How we use information

  • To perform reverse image search and show Visual / Exact / Product-style matches
  • To generate optional “About” / insight text when AI features are enabled
  • To enforce free search limits and Pro subscription entitlements
  • To show ads to free users and measure ad performance
  • To send service notifications (updates, reminders) if you opt in
  • To improve stability, understand feature usage, and fix bugs
  • To comply with law and prevent abuse (harassment, illegal content, fraud)

5. Images & search processing

When you start a search, your image may be processed as follows:

  1. On-device selection — you pick a gallery photo or take a camera photo.
  2. Temporary hosting (when required) — some search paths briefly upload the image to a temporary public file host so a reverse-search provider (for example Google Lens web flows) can fetch it. Uploads are for search completion, not long-term storage by us.
  3. Search providers — image data or a hosted URL may be sent to search backends configured for the App (for example Apify Google Lens actors, Bing Visual Search, or similar services used by the App build).
  4. Optional AI insights — if enabled in your build, image data may be sent to OpenAI (e.g. GPT-4o mini) and/or Qwen-VL (DashScope) solely to produce descriptions / insights shown in the App.

Result pages often open third-party websites. Those sites may set cookies or collect data under their own policies.

6. Device permissions

Depending on your OS version, the App may request:

Permission Purpose
Internet / network Search, ads, analytics, subscriptions, loading result images
Camera Capture a photo to search
System photo picker (no broad gallery permission) On Android 13+, gallery images are chosen via the OS photo picker without READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO
Notifications Optional push messages (Android 13+ requires runtime consent)
Billing (Google Play) Purchase and restore Pro subscriptions

You can deny or revoke permissions in system settings. Some features will not work without the related permission.

7. Third-party services

We use trusted processors to run the App. Their handling of data is governed by their policies:

  • Google Play / Google Play Billing — subscription purchase, restore, and entitlement
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — ads for free users
  • Firebase (Google) — Analytics and Cloud Messaging when enabled
  • Search / hosting providers — temporary image hosts and reverse-search backends configured for the App
  • AI providers — OpenAI and/or Alibaba Cloud DashScope (Qwen) when insight features are enabled
  • Apple App Store — purchases on iOS builds, if distributed there

We encourage you to review: Google Privacy Policy, OpenAI Privacy Policy, and other provider policies applicable to your region.

8. Advertising

Free users may see banner, native, interstitial, or rewarded ads served by Google AdMob. Ad partners may use advertising IDs and approximate device signals to serve and measure ads.

Pro subscribers generally do not see ads while entitlement is active. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example Google advertising settings on Android).

9. Subscriptions & payments

Paid plans (weekly / monthly / yearly product IDs configured in the store) are sold through Google Play Billing (and Apple on iOS). We do not receive or store your full card numbers. Google/Apple process payment details under their terms.

The App may store an encrypted local record of Pro status, plan type, expiry, and remaining search allowance on your device so features work offline. Restore Purchases re-syncs with the store when needed.

10. Storage & retention

  • On device: history, favorites, settings, and subscription cache until you clear them or uninstall the App
  • Temporary uploads: removed according to the temporary host’s retention (often hours); we do not keep a permanent image archive
  • Analytics / ads / FCM: retained by those providers per their policies and your OS controls
  • Purchase records: retained by Google Play / Apple as required for billing and tax

11. Security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate for a mobile app, including encrypted local storage for subscription entitlement data and HTTPS for network calls where supported by providers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please keep your device locked and updated.

12. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Do not use the App to seek or share sexual content involving minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided data through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

13. Your choices & rights

  • Deny camera, photos, or notification permissions in system settings
  • Clear in-app history / favorites, or uninstall the App to remove local data
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play (or App Store) subscription settings
  • Opt out of personalized ads via your Google account / device ad settings
  • Depending on your region (for example GDPR/CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete, or restrict certain processing — contact us to make a request

14. International transfers

Providers may process data in the United States or other countries. Where required, they use appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) described in their documentation.
